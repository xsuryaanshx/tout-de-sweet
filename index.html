Born from French Rigor,
Rooted in Dubai's
Quiet Sanctuaries.

Tout De Sweet was conceived not as an ordinary bakery, but as a dedicated atelier — an architectural tribute to the grand pastry salons of Paris, nestled within the quiet residential courtyards of Al Safa. We work exclusively with single-origin Grand Cru cacao from Valrhona, cultured butter from the Charentes-Poitou region, and heritage flours stone-milled in France.
